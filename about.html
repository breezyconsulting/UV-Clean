/ About

A different kind of cleaning company — built around light, not chemicals.

UV Clean was founded on a simple premise: that the most powerful sanitiser in the universe — short-wave ultraviolet light — should be available to ordinary Australian businesses and homes, not locked away in hospital basements.

/ Mission

Clean shouldn't cost you chemicals.

For decades, "clean" has meant a cupboard full of sprays, fumes, residues and disposable wipes. It means surfaces that smell clean — but harbour what spray bottles can't reach. It means waiting hours before a room is safe to use again.

We saw a better way. The Sterile-Bright™ STRL-PU-250WUV is hand-portable, hospital-grade, and capable of sterilising a vehicle interior in under a minute. It uses zero chemicals. It leaves zero residue. And the second the cycle ends, the space is ready.

Our job is to bring that technology to every kind of space that needs to be genuinely clean — fleets, clinics, gyms, hotels, homes, and everything in between.

/ Where We Come From

Noosa Heads, Queensland.

UV Clean is run out of Noosa Heads on Queensland's Sunshine Coast — a part of the world where clean air, clean water and clean spaces aren't luxuries, they're the standard.

From our base we cover the Sunshine Coast as routine — Noosa, Maroochydore, Mooloolaba, Caloundra, Nambour, Coolum, the lot — and travel to Brisbane and the Gold Coast on a near-weekly basis.

For multi-site commercial contracts we mobilise nationally. If you operate a fleet, clinic network, or hospitality group anywhere in Australia, we can come to you.
