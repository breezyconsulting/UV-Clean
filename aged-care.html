Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

Can residents remain in the facility during treatment?

Yes — residents are simply moved out of the specific room or area being treated. Treatment is fast (typically under 15 minutes per room) and the space is safe to re-enter immediately after the unit is powered down.

Will UV-C damage furniture, fittings, or equipment?

At our exposure times, no. UV-C affects DNA-level pathogens, not material structure. Prolonged exposure can fade some plastics over years of daily use — not relevant for periodic disinfection.

How does this fit with our existing cleaning contract?

We work alongside, not instead of, your cleaning team. UV-C is added at the end of a standard clean to kill what wiping can leave behind — particularly in cracks, on porous surfaces, and on items that are awkward to wipe.

Can you respond to an active outbreak after hours?

Yes. We carry overnight and weekend response. Call 0417 631 743 directly for outbreak callouts.
