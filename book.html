/ Preparation

What to do before we arrive.

A small amount of preparation lets us focus on the disinfection work rather than the logistics. None of this is mandatory, but it speeds things up.

  1. 1

    Clear obvious clutter

    UV-C reaches around objects, but a clear surface treats faster and more thoroughly. Move papers, food, and personal items aside where practical.

  2. 2

    Remove plants and live animals

    Plants tolerate brief UV-C exposure but are happier elsewhere. Pets and fish must be relocated for the cycle — no exceptions.

  3. 3

    Cover anything heat- or light-sensitive

    Long-exposed-photo prints, valuable artwork, and certain plastic-aged display items benefit from a light cover. Most household and office items are fine as-is.

  4. 4

    Brief your team

    For workplaces, everyone needs to be out of the treatment zone for the cycle duration. We coordinate this with whoever holds keys or runs the floor.

  5. 5

    Note any specific concerns

    Recent illness in a particular room, known contamination event, area that's been a problem — tell us. We can extend dwell time on focal zones.

/ After Treatment

What happens once we leave.

UV-C treatment ends the moment the unit powers down. Unlike chemical treatments there's no waiting, no airing-out, no surface to wipe over.

Immediate re-entry

The space is safe to occupy the second the cycle ends. No off-gassing, no residue, no smell.

No surface re-wipe

You don't need to clean afterward. The treatment is complete — wiping over would only reintroduce contamination.

Treatment log delivered

Within one business day you'll receive a per-zone log: date, dwell time, operator, signature. Use it for audits, accreditation, or customer communication.

Maintain results

UV-C disinfects what's there at treatment time. Regular cadence (weekly/fortnightly/monthly) maintains the lower microbial baseline; one-off treatments are point-in-time.

/ Booking Questions

Common booking questions, answered.

How far in advance should I book?

Sunshine Coast bookings are typically available same-day or next-day. Brisbane and Gold Coast 24–48 hours. For weekend or after-hours commercial work, allow a few days. Outbreak response is exempt from advance booking — call directly.

Do you offer cancellation refunds?

Cancellations more than 24 hours before the scheduled time receive a full refund. Within 24 hours we charge 50% of the booked fee to cover the reserved time. No-shows are charged in full.

Can I be present during treatment?

Not in the treatment zone itself — the 250W unit's output is industrial and direct human exposure is not permitted. You can absolutely remain on the premises in another room; we operate the unit by wireless key fob from outside the zone.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, card payment on site, and 7-day invoice terms for established commercial accounts. Contract clients are typically billed monthly in advance.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the treatment is delivered to documented exposure standards — i.e. the right dose, in the right zones, for the right dwell time. We can't guarantee zero re-contamination after we leave, because that depends on what re-enters the space. What we deliver is a documented baseline reset.
