/ FAQ

Questions, plainly answered.

Is UV sanitisation safe for my space?

+

Absolutely. The cycle runs in an unoccupied space — humans, pets and plants are kept out for the duration. The moment the cycle ends, there's zero residue, no off-gassing, and the space is immediately safe to re-enter. Far safer than the chemical alternative.

What can UV-C actually kill?

+

A 250-watt broad-spectrum unit at close range neutralises bacteria, viruses, mould spores, fungi, and most common surface pathogens by disrupting their DNA and RNA. We're powerful enough to be deployed inside ambulances — the same logic applies to your space.

How long does a treatment take?

+

A vehicle interior is done in under a minute. A standard office or hotel room takes 3–8 minutes. Larger spaces are treated in zones. Setup and pack-down add a few minutes — overall, most jobs are start-to-finish in well under half an hour.

Do I need to be there during the treatment?

+

No — and you shouldn't be in the room during the cycle itself. We coordinate access, complete the work, and confirm when the space is handed back. For commercial clients we offer after-hours and weekend schedules.

How is this different from a regular clean?

+

Regular cleaning removes visible dirt. UV sanitisation neutralises what you can't see — at a microbial level — without chemicals, residues, or wait times. The two are complements, not substitutes. We pair beautifully with your existing cleaning regime.

Where do you service?

+

We're based in Noosa Heads and cover the entire Sunshine Coast as our home turf — Maroochydore, Caloundra, Nambour, Mooloolaba, the lot. For larger commercial contracts we mobilise to Brisbane, the Gold Coast, and across Australia.
