/ Compared To

What you're stepping up from.

Many businesses already use a "UV cleaner" — usually a 30–40 watt fluorescent unit, often consumer-grade. The Sterile-Bright is a different category of equipment entirely.

Traditional 40W Fluorescent UV

What you're replacing

  • Approximately 40W output — adequate for surface sanitisation only at close range and long dwell
  • Narrow 254nm wavelength only — misses broader pathogen profile
  • Directional output requires manual repositioning around the room
  • 10–30 minute cycles for a single room
  • Fragile glass tube construction — limited durability for portable use
  • Often consumer-grade with no compliance certification

Sterile-Bright STRL-PU-250WUV

What you're moving to

  • 250W output — six times the photon dose, dramatically shorter cycles
  • Broad-spectrum 180–456nm — covers DNA-damage UV-C plus disinfection-relevant UV-A/B
  • 360° omnidirectional — full room coverage from a single position
  • Under 60 seconds for a vehicle, 3–12 minutes for a room
  • Industrial build for daily commercial deployment
  • IEC 62471 certified, CE marked, ICNIRP exposure compliant

/ Purchase Questions

Answers before you commit.

What's the typical payback period?

Depends entirely on use volume. A car dealership treating 20 vehicles a day pays the unit back in roughly 6–9 months versus contracted treatment cost. An aged care facility treating common areas weekly pays back in 12–18 months. We can run the numbers for your specific operation.

Can we finance it?

Yes — equipment finance and operating lease arrangements are available through our finance partners. Typical 36-month terms for commercial buyers. Talk to us when you request your quote.

How many units does my business need?

Most multi-site operations run one unit per site, with a centrally-deployable spare for outbreak response and fleet treatment days. Very large facilities (major hotels, hospital networks) may need two or three units to keep treatment cadence on schedule.

Is bulb replacement difficult?

No — replacement is a 5-minute task without tools. Bulbs are rated at 9,000 hours; for a typical commercial buyer that's 3–5 years of daily use.

Can we get a demo before buying?

Yes. We routinely arrange a free demo treatment of one of your spaces so you can see the unit operate, measure cycle time in your environment, and ask questions. Sunshine Coast demos are typically same-week.

Do you ship interstate?

Yes — Australia-wide. Training is conducted via video for interstate buyers, or in person for orders of two or more units.
