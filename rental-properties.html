Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

How long does treatment take per property?

Typical 1-bedroom apartment: 15–20 minutes. 3-bedroom house: 30–45 minutes. We treat in parallel with your cleaning team — they handle linen and visible surfaces, we handle disinfection.

Will guests smell anything when they arrive?

No. UV-C is a light-based treatment — no chemical, no fragrance, no residue. The property smells like the property.

Can you treat between back-to-back same-day bookings?

Yes, this is one of our most common use cases. We coordinate with your turnover cleaner to fit treatment into the changeover window.

Do you service property managers with multiple listings?

Yes — preferred rates for portfolios. Contact us for a quote.
